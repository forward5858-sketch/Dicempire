Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Dicempire (multiplayer version)

In short

Dicempire is an online game you play with friends, each on their own phone. So that you can play, the player name you enter and your moves in the game are sent to the game's server and shown to the other players in your room. This information is kept only in the server's memory while the game is running and is never written to a database. There are no accounts, ads or analytics tools.

What is sent to the server

  • Player name: The name you enter in the lobby (up to 14 characters). Everyone in the room can see it. You don't have to use your real name; a nickname works fine.
  • Room information: The 4-character room code, the state of the game and your moves. The server rolls the dice and runs the game.
  • Session key: A random key issued by the server so that you can return to the same seat if you reload the page or briefly lose your connection. It does not identify who you are.

All of this is kept only in the server's memory and is deleted when the room closes. A room closes when nobody has been connected for about 1 minute in the lobby or 10 minutes during a game, when everyone leaves the room, or when the server restarts. No game history, scores or statistics are stored.

The game itself does not record IP addresses. However, the hosting service that runs the game's server may, like with any website, keep technical logs such as IP addresses and request times for a period of time for security and operational purposes, under its own policies.

What is stored on your device

  • The player name you last entered and the game language you chose are kept in your browser's persistent storage so they are ready next time.
  • The room code and session key are kept only while that tab is open, so you can return to the game after reloading the page; they are deleted when you close the tab.

You can remove all of this by clearing your browser data.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts: The game's fonts are loaded from Google's servers. As with any website visit, your browser sends your IP address and browser information to Google when this happens. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Sharing invites: The "Invite your friends", "WhatsApp" and "Copy link" buttons only work when you tap them. The invite link and room code are handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) for you to send; that app's own privacy rules apply.

Apart from this, no data is shared with anyone else, sold or used for advertising.

Permissions

On devices that support it, your phone vibrates briefly when it's your turn. When you copy the invite link, only the link is written to the clipboard; nothing is read from it. The game does not access your location, camera, microphone, files or contacts. The advertising ID is not used.

Children

Dicempire is suitable for all ages. Because player names are visible to the other players in the room, we recommend that children use a nickname instead of their real name. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
