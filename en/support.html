When reporting a bug, it helps to include your device model, Android or iOS version, the game version (shown in your device's app info screen), the room code and what you did right before the problem. A screenshot helps too.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play alone?

Yes. Tap "Play vs computer" on the main menu; in the lobby you can add up to 3 computer opponents with "Add computer player". Friends can join the same room too.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. The server rolls the dice and runs the game, so you need a connection even when playing against the computer.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, neither.

My connection dropped. What happens?

When your connection comes back, the game puts you back in the same seat automatically. If it's your turn and you don't return within 45 seconds, your turn is skipped and the game continues for the others.

Someone I don't know joined my room.

Only people who know the room code can join. The host can remove a player in the lobby with the ✕ next to their name. You can also create a new room and send the code only to your friends.

How do I delete my data?

Your player name and moves stay in the server's memory only while the room is open and are deleted when it closes. You can remove the name and language saved on your device by deleting the app. Details are in the privacy policy.
