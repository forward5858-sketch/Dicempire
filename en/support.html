Contact us

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your device model, Android or iOS version, the game version (shown in your device's app info screen) and what you did right before the problem. A screenshot helps too.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The store versions never connect to the internet. The web version only downloads its files on first launch.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, neither.

Where is my unfinished game?

Your unfinished game is saved on your device. Close and reopen the app to continue where you left off.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your progress lives on your device, not on a server. If your phone's backup is on (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone), it usually moves to a new device restored from that backup. On the web, saves belong to that browser.

How do I reset my statistics?

Use the "Reset statistics" button in Settings. This cannot be undone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app; on the web, clear your browser data. We hold no data about you. Details are in the privacy policy.
