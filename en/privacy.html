Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Dicempire (iOS, Android and web)

In short

Dicempire is an online dice and island war game you play with friends or against the computer. So that you can play, the player name you enter and your moves are sent to the game's server and shown to the other players in your room. This is kept only in the server's memory while the game is running and is never written to a database. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools; we do not track you across apps or websites.

What is sent to the server

  • Player name: The name you enter (up to 14 characters). Everyone in the room can see it. You don't have to use your real name; a nickname works fine.
  • Room information: The 4-character room code, the state of the game and your moves. The server rolls the dice and runs the game. Computer opponents also play on the server.
  • Session key: A random key issued by the server so you can return to the same seat if your connection drops briefly. It does not identify who you are.

All of this is kept only in the server's memory and is deleted when the room closes. A room closes when nobody has been connected for about 1 minute in the lobby or 10 minutes during a game, when all players leave, or when the server restarts. No game history, scores or statistics are stored.

The game itself does not record IP addresses. The game's server runs on Render (render.com) in a data centre in the European Union (Frankfurt). Like any internet service, Render may keep technical logs such as IP addresses and request times for a period of time for security and operations, under its own policies.

What is stored on your device

  • The player name you last entered and the language you chose are kept on your device (in the app's or browser's own storage) so they are ready next time.
  • The room code and session key are kept only while the game is open and are deleted when you close the app or tab.

You can remove all of this by deleting the app or clearing your browser data. Because we hold no lasting record about you, there is nothing else to request deletion of.

Third parties

  • Fonts and files: All of the game's files, fonts included, ship with the game or load from the game's own server. Nothing is loaded from other servers.
  • Sharing invites: The "Invite your friends", "WhatsApp" and "Copy link" buttons only work when you tap them. The invite link and room code are handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp) for you to send; that app's own privacy rules apply.
  • These pages: The privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical logs too (GitHub privacy statement).

Apart from this, no data is shared with anyone, sold or used for advertising.

Permissions

Your phone vibrates briefly when it's your turn. When you copy the invite link, only the link is written to the clipboard; nothing is read from it. The app goes online only to connect to the game's server. Location, camera, microphone, photos, files and contacts are never accessed. The advertising ID is not used and no tracking permission is requested.

Children

Dicempire is suitable for all ages. Because player names are visible to the other players in the room, we recommend that children use a nickname instead of their real name. Only people who know the room code can join a room, and the host can remove a player they don't want. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
