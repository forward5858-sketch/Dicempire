Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Dicempire (Android, iOS and web)

In short

Dicempire does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting tools, accounts or servers. It does not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

To work, the game keeps the following only on your device, in your browser's or the app's own storage:

  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, speed, theme)
  • Your unfinished game
  • Your statistics and achievements
  • Player names you type on the setup screen

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. If your phone's own backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad), your device may include it in the backup in your account; only you control that backup and we cannot see it. You can remove everything by deleting the app or clearing your browser data. The "Reset statistics" button in Settings also deletes statistics and achievements.

Internet connection

The Google Play and App Store versions ship with all their files and never connect to the internet. The web version downloads its own files on first launch and then works offline. No files, fonts included, are loaded from third-party servers.

Permissions

No permissions are used other than vibration (on devices that support it) and keeping the screen awake during play. The internet permission listed for the Android version is a technical requirement of the web view that displays the game; the app itself blocks every outgoing request. The iOS version requests no permissions and does not show a tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency), because it does not track. Location, camera, microphone, photos, files and contacts are never accessed. The advertising ID is not used.

Links

Links inside the game, such as this page and the support page, open in your device's own browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web server, GitHub may keep technical logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

Dicempire is suitable for all ages, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated. The Turkish version is the original; if the two differ, the Turkish version applies.

Contact

For questions, reach us through the options on the support page.
